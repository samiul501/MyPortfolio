About

        
I break things so users don't have to

        

          

            
I'm a Junior Software QA Engineer at AKIJ iBOS Limited in Dhaka. I test Managerium ERP, Managerium POS, BMA and NextJobz, covering manual, API and performance testing on everything from business workflows to critical modules before they reach production.

            
My strength is careful manual and functional testing backed by a solid grasp of SDLC and STLC. I write test plans, test cases, checklists and bug reports, validate APIs with Postman and Newman, and load-test with JMeter and k6.

            
Right now I'm going deeper into test automation and AI-assisted QA, with Playwright, agent-driven frameworks and autonomous API testing. I hold a BSc in CSE from Green University of Bangladesh, where I researched word embeddings (CBOW & Skip-Gram).

          

          

            
Manual & Functional
Test case design, execution, regression, UAT and root-cause analysis across ERP and POS flows.

            
API Testing
Postman collections, Newman runs and assertions on status, data and response time.

            
Performance
Load, stress and user-journey tests with JMeter and k6, with HTML reports.

            
Automation & AI
Playwright and Selenium with POM, plus AI agents that generate and run tests.
